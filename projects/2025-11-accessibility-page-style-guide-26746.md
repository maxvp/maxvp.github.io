Established a new section in the Style Guide defining accessibility standards to ensure documentation meets the [European Accessibility Act (EAA)](https://ec.europa.eu/social/main.jsp?catId=1202) compliance guidelines. This initiative improves the quality of AI-powered contributions and ensures content is accessible to all users.
